Video Batcher 
Turn one video into hundreds of unique, ready-to-post variations — in minutes, right on your own computer.

Video Batcher Pro is a professional desktop app for content creators, marketers, and social media teams who repurpose content at scale. Feed it your videos or images, choose your settings, and it generates hundreds of one-of-a-kind variations — each with its own subtle look, sound, captions, and file fingerprint.

Why Video Batcher?
Every export is truly unique. 18+ randomized effects (brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, zoom, speed, rotation, color temperature, sharpening, audio pitch/EQ, and more) are applied within professional limits — variations look and sound natural, never over-processed.
Built for volume. Batch up to 12 source files at once, run parallel workers, and let GPU acceleration (NVIDIA / AMD / Intel / Apple Silicon) do the heavy lifting.
100% local & private. All processing happens on your machine. Your content is never uploaded to any cloud.
Key Features
🎬 Randomized Visual Effects — Set your own min/max ranges for 18+ effects; every variation gets its own random mix within your limits.

✍️ Caption Overlays — Build a caption library and stamp a random caption on each variation. Full styling control: fonts, colors, outlines, shadows, background boxes, drag-to-position on a live preview — with full color-emoji support 🎉

🎵 Audio Pool — Add a library of music/audio tracks and let each variation pull a random one. Replace or mix with the original audio, trim tracks, duck the original, randomize start points — and preview the exact mix before rendering.

📱 Device Metadata Profiles — Give every export polished, camera-style metadata. Choose from popular device profiles (iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, and more) or let each variation pick a random one, so your files carry clean, professional capture details instead of editing-software tags.

🔄 Fresh Metadata on Every File — Original file metadata is cleaned and re-randomized per variation, so every output stands on its own.

🖱️ Drag & Drop Everything — Drop videos or images anywhere on the window. Supports MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, JPG, PNG, WEBP, and BMP.

📋 Presets & Detailed Logs — Save your favorite configurations, and get a JSON report of exactly what was applied to every variation.
